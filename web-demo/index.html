About this version

AI models on this device

YuNet (MIT), MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache-2.0), FER+ (MIT) and SFace (Apache-2.0) are the same model files as the full HealthVision AI app, run here with ONNX Runtime Web and MediaPipe in your browser.

Production readiness gate NOT MET

  • Models are not yet validated on local, consented data.
  • Fairness across skin tones, ages and lighting is not yet evaluated.
  • Recognition thresholds are demo values, not calibrated.
  • Liveness (photo or screen spoof detection) is not implemented.
  • No security, privacy or clinical review has taken place yet.

Differences from the full app

  • Camera works when this page is opened as a normal website (for example GitHub Pages). Inside an embedded viewer the camera may be blocked; use photo upload there.
  • No accounts, history, PDF reports or audit log. Results live in this tab and disappear on reload.
  • The detector runs on a 640×640 letterboxed copy of your photo, so boxes can differ by a few pixels from the full app.
